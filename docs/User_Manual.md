# University Appointment System - User Manual

This manual provides a comprehensive guide on how to use the University Appointment System for the three main user roles: **Students (Users)**, **Service Providers (Staff)**, and **Administrators**.

---

## 1. Getting Started (All Users)

### Registration and Login
1. **Download/Open the App:** Access the app via the provided web link or install the mobile application on your Android/iOS device.
2. **Sign Up:** If you are a new user, click "Register". Fill in your Name, Email, Password, and select your Role (Student or Provider). 
   - *Note for Providers:* You may need to upload verification documents. Your account will require approval by an Admin before you can offer services.
3. **Login:** Enter your email and password to access your dashboard.

---

## 2. Student Guide

As a student, you can use the app to book appointments with various university departments and staff members.

### Booking an Appointment
1. Navigate to the **Home/Dashboard** to see a list of available services.
2. Tap on a service to view details, including the provider's name, duration, and whether it is a virtual or in-person meeting.
3. Click **Book Appointment** and select an available date and time from the calendar.
4. Add any relevant notes and confirm your booking.

### Managing Appointments
- Go to the **My Appointments** tab to view your upcoming, pending, and past appointments.
- If your appointment is marked as **Virtual**, a "Join Video Call" button will appear at the scheduled time. Clicking this will open the built-in video conferencing tool (Jitsi).
- For **In-Person** appointments, you can access a QR code from the appointment details to present for quick check-in upon arrival.

### Communication & Feedback
- **Messages:** Use the in-app chat to message the provider if you have questions before your appointment.
- **Notifications:** You will receive push notifications regarding appointment approvals, cancellations, and reminders.
- **Reviews:** After an appointment is completed, you can leave a star rating and comment for the provider to help improve university services.

---

## 3. Provider Guide

As a service provider (e.g., Academic Advisor, Health Center Staff), you manage your availability and handle student requests.

### Setting Up Your Profile
1. Navigate to your **Profile** or **Settings**.
2. Set your **Work Hours** by defining your start and end times for each day of the week, and mark any days you are completely off.

### Creating Services
1. Go to the **Manage Services** screen.
2. Click **Add Service** to define what you offer (e.g., "Academic Advising 101").
3. Set the duration and toggle whether the service is conducted virtually.

### Managing Appointments
- **Calendar View:** Use the Calendar screen to get a visual overview of your daily and weekly schedule.
- **Approvals:** When a student books an appointment, it will appear as "Pending". You must review and **Approve** or **Cancel** it.
- **Emergency Absence:** If you have an unexpected emergency, you can use the "Emergency Absence" feature to automatically cancel all appointments for the day and notify affected students.

### Analytics & Tools
- **Analytics Dashboard:** View statistics on your most popular services, completion rates, and average ratings from students.
- **QR Scanner:** Use the built-in QR Scanner to quickly check-in students who arrive for in-person appointments.

---

## 4. Administrator Guide

Administrators oversee the entire system, ensuring smooth operation and managing user permissions.

### User Management
- Navigate to the **User Management** screen from the Admin Dashboard.
- **Verify Providers:** Review newly registered Provider accounts and verify their documents. Unverified providers cannot create services.
- **Role Assignment:** You have the authority to change user roles (e.g., promoting a Staff member to an Admin).

### System Oversight
- **All Appointments:** Admins can view a master list of all appointments across the university to monitor usage.
- **Dashboard Stats:** View high-level metrics such as total users, total appointments, and active services to gauge system health.
